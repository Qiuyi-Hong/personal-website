# Personal website

Qiuyi Hong's personal website at qiuyihong.com: a recruiter-facing profile forked from chanhdai.com and rebuilt with Qiuyi's own content and identity.

## Language

### Sources

**Reference site**:
chanhdai.com (and its repo ncdai/chanhdai.com), which the new site forks; its code is MIT but its name, marks and likeness are not ours. Its repo is the fork's upstream.
_Avoid_: template, base

**Old site**:
The current qiuyihong.com (repo Qiuyi-Hong/personal-portfolio-website) that the new site replaces; a source of voice and project write-ups.
_Avoid_: previous portfolio, v1

**LinkedIn snapshot**:
A dated, verbatim capture of Qiuyi's LinkedIn profile; the source of truth for facts such as roles, dates and education.
_Avoid_: LinkedIn export, profile dump

**Content pack**:
The reconciled, canonical content for every panel, drawn from the old site and the LinkedIn snapshot.
_Avoid_: data, copy, content files

### Page

**Panel**:
One bordered section of the single-column homepage (e.g. experience, publications), separated from its neighbours by screen lines or stripe dividers.
_Avoid_: section, card, block

**Profile header**:
The top panel naming Qiuyi, giving the headline, and carrying the CV, email and LinkedIn calls to action.
_Avoid_: hero, banner

**Visual identity**:
The mark, accent colour and type pairing that make the site read as Qiuyi's rather than the reference site's.
_Avoid_: branding, theme
